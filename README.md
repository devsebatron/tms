**KIN** is an online **Task Management software** that assists teams in managing their work in real time. Assign and prioritize tasks, create recurring tasks, and provide teams with a collaborative platform to share files and comments. More than just a to-do list, our application tracks your chores in real-time to keep you on track. Several mind-calming activities are available for employees to engage in during downtime, hence increasing productivity.
There are four user logins available in the software: **Admin/Super-User:** has the authority to control every user's function within the software, **Department Head:** has the right to assign a Project Manager and add/remove employees from their department, **Project Manager (PM):**  has the right to add/remove employees to a particular project and assign tasks with deadlines. They can also track the progress of the project, **Employees:-** Can View/keep track of the project assigned to them. Will have an allotted time for recreational activities to restore energy and keep the work flowing. This software is designed for small and medium-sized enterprises in order to improve internal functionality, task collaboration, and work organization.
